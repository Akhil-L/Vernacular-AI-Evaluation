# Evaluation Prompts

## Scenario 1 — Festival Sale Poster

### Hindi
A festive Diwali sale promotional poster for a small Indian grocery (kirana) store. Warm, vibrant style with diyas, string lights, marigold motifs. Orange/gold/deep-red palette. Portrait orientation, suitable for print and WhatsApp sharing. Display large, legible text in Hindi script: आनंद किराना स्टोर, दिवाली सेल, and सभी वस्तुओं पर 20% की छूट. Store name at top, offer text prominent in a bold banner at bottom.

## Scenario 2 — Everyday Shop Signboard

### Hindi
A shop signboard for a small Indian tailoring shop, meant to be mounted outside. Clean professional signage style — bold sans-serif, blue/white or maroon/white, simple border. Landscape orientation. Display large legible text in Hindi script: रवि टेलर्स, सिलाई, अल्टरेशन, ब्लाउज़ का काम, and सुबह 9 बजे से रात 8 बजे तक. Shop name prominent at the top, services clearly visible in the middle, and business hours clearly visible at the bottom.
