Scale controls: rater qualification, language pools, gold checks, disagreement monitoring, automated duplicate detection, payout tracking, prompt/model versioning and quota dashboards.

Key findings

  • ChatGPT led in both languages, but the gap was smaller in Telugu than Hindi.
  • Telugu festival posters were the closest context: Gemini beat ChatGPT 60% to 40% among 10 decisive votes.
  • Product cards produced the clearest separation: ChatGPT 95.5% scenario win rate; ChatGPT beat Gemini 90% to 10% head-to-head.
Important limitation: the brief required OpenAI GPT Image 1, Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Image and Google Gemini 3.1 Flash Image Preview. Those exact models were not accessible through the free user-facing interfaces used for this pilot, so the dashboard names the exact models actually evaluated.
